How to play

  1. Both players start at square 0.
  2. Take turns rolling the dice.
  3. Land on a ladder to climb; land on a snake to slide down.
  4. You must roll the exact number needed to reach 100.
  5. The first player to reach 100 wins!
OTXS tip: Play together while travelling, waiting or relaxing with friends.
