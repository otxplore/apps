How to play

⌨️ Desktop: Use arrow keys or W, A, S, D.

📱 Touchscreen: Swipe on the board or use the directional buttons.

🔢 Merge: Matching tiles combine into their sum. Each tile can merge only once per move.

♾️ Endless: Reaching 2048 is only the beginning. Continue to 4096, 8192, and beyond!

↶ Undo: Reverse your last valid move once, then make another move.
