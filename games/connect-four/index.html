How to play

  1. Player 1 uses red; Player 2 uses yellow.
  2. Take turns selecting one of the seven columns.
  3. Your disc drops into the lowest available space.
  4. Connect four discs horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.
  5. If all 42 spaces fill with no winner, the match is a draw.
Strategy tip: Keep an eye on your opponent's three-disc lines. Sometimes the best move is to block rather than attack.

Your game runs locally in your browser. No account, server or external library is needed.
