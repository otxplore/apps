How to play

  1. Player 1 uses X; Player 2 uses O.
  2. Tap an empty square to place your symbol.
  3. Connect three horizontally, vertically or diagonally to win.
  4. If all nine squares fill without a winner, it's a draw.
OTXS tip: Best played with a friend while travelling or relaxing together.
