How to play
• Roll a 6 to bring a token out of base.
• A 6 gives you another roll.
• Land on a single opponent to send it home.
• Star squares are safe from capture.
• Reach the centre with an exact roll.
• Get all four tokens home to win.

Local multiplayer: four players share this device. Pass it to the next player after your turn.
